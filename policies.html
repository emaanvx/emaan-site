House rules

Short version: argue with me, don't insult anyone, and tell me when I'm wrong.

What's welcome here

Questions, including the ones you think are too basic. Disagreement, especially from people actually doing the work. Better ideas than mine, which I will take and credit.

If you think a claim on this site is wrong, say so. I would rather be corrected in public than be wrong in public for longer.

What gets removed

Spam, harassment, and personal attacks. Anything aimed at someone's identity rather than their argument. Pitches disguised as questions. I remove those without notice and without a debate about it.

If I get something wrong

Tell me. I fix the post and leave a dated correction note at the bottom saying what changed, so anyone who read the old version can see what moved. I don't quietly edit and pretend it was always right, and I don't delete the post to make the mistake disappear. If you spotted it, you get the credit in the note unless you'd rather not.

Sponsorship and gifts

Nobody pays me to write here. No affiliate links, no sponsored posts, no gifted gear in exchange for coverage.

I am paid by the clients I direct for, and some of that work informs what I write here. That is a commercial relationship and I say so wherever it is relevant. If a post is ever paid for by anyone, the first line of that post will say so before you read anything else.

How to reach me

Email emaan@venture-x.ca. I read everything and I answer most of it, usually late. If it's about working together, say so in the subject line and send the work with it.

When I post

Irregularly, and on purpose. I'd rather publish something worth the read every few weeks than something thin every Monday.

How I use AI

I use AI tools every working day, so a page saying "I use AI thoughtfully" would be worthless. Here is the specific version.

Where I use AI

  • Research and leads. Finding what has been written on a subject, and finding the original source of a figure somebody quoted at me.
  • Structure. Outlines, and arguing with me about the order of an argument before I write it.
  • Editing. Checking a finished draft against my own voice guide and strike list, and handing me back a numbered list of problems. It finds the sentence I was proud of that says nothing.
  • The small mechanical pieces. Alt text, meta descriptions, tags, and headline options I then rewrite.
  • Asset iteration in commercial work. Generative tools for some graphics and for exploring concepts before a shoot. Where that is true of a specific piece, it is said on that piece.

Where I don't

  • The opinions are mine. If this site claims something is true about how video works, I believe it, and I am the one who will be wrong if it is not.
  • The final sentences are mine. AI suggests; I write. I do not publish a paragraph I did not type.
  • No AI-generated footage passed off as real work. Nothing here that looks like a client shoot is anything but a client shoot.
  • No AI-generated images of real people, including me.
  • No fabricated numbers. If I cannot source a figure, it does not appear — not as an estimate, not as a round number, not as "roughly".

How I check

I open every source myself before I cite it. If a tool hands me a fact, I find it at its original source before it goes anywhere, and if I cannot find it there, the claim comes out of the draft rather than getting softened. I have had an assistant hand me a plausible, well-formatted, entirely invented statistic more than once. That is the failure mode to design around, and it is why the rule is "open the source", not "double-check".

Who's accountable

Me. If it is on this site, I published it, and I will correct it in public. An agent's mistakes are mine, not its own.

Tools I use

Claude, for research, editing feedback, and as an agent working directly in the folder that runs this site. Obsidian for drafting. Various generative image tools inside commercial projects, disclosed on the work itself.
