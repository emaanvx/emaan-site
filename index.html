03 Mentorship

I take on a few people at a time, and I work with them properly.

Not a course. Not a community you pay to be ignored in. You bring real work — a cut you can't fix, a client you're underpricing, a reel that died — and we take it apart. I tell you what I'd actually do, which is usually the thing you were avoiding.

The number stays small because the only version of this worth doing is the one where I remember your last three pieces without looking them up.

Who it's for

  • You can already shoot and cut. The craft isn't the problem.
  • You're charging less than the work is worth and you know it.
  • You want to direct, not just operate.
  • You'll send the work before you've made it presentable.

Who it isn't for

  • Anyone looking for a gear list or a preset pack.
  • Anyone who wants the follower count without the craft underneath it.
  • Anyone who needs to be told they're doing well.
  • Anyone who wants it done for them. That's a different engagement.

One next step.

Send me the work you're least happy with and one sentence on why. If I think I can help, we'll talk. If I don't, I'll say so, and that'll be the more useful answer.
