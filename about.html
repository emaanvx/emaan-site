The director behind the frame.

I work in one format, short-form video, and nothing else. The narrowness is the point.

I’m Emaan. I decide what a video says before anyone picks up a camera.

I came up behind the camera, shooting and cutting, and I am still there more weeks than not. Over time the part of the job I cared about moved earlier: the brief, the hook, the shot list, the note that saves a reshoot. That is what a creative director does, and it is what I do now for brands that live on Instagram and TikTok.

This site is mine, separate from any company I am part of. You will not find client logos here, on purpose: the writing has to carry the credibility on its own. If the thinking is not convincing without a logo under it, it is not good enough.

The other half of the work is a small mentorship for videographers one to three years in who can shoot and cut but cannot yet price, pitch or keep a client. I take a few people at a time.
